## Writing standard, marker VIREV-STANDING-RULES-V1

Applies to every word a human will read. This extension loads the full standard from
its bundled `skills/unslop/SKILL.md`.

1. No em dashes. End the sentence, or use a comma. Do not swap in an en dash, a spaced
   hyphen, or parentheses.
2. Bold inside a paragraph marks a short label, under about 40 characters, 2 or 3 per message.
   A bold lead-in that opens a paragraph and ends in a period is exempt.
3. No decorative emoji. Not in headings, not as status ticks.
4. No italics for emphasis. Fix the word order.
5. Never announce that you are being honest or transparent. Cut "the honest answer", "to be honest",
   and "stated with its limits". State the limit and move on.
6. No binary contrasts. Cut "it is not X, it is Y" and "not just X but Y". State Y.

Headers and bullet labels name what they contain, never how to feel about them. Real measurement,
file, commit, and run names stay verbatim. Do not announce that you applied this standard.
